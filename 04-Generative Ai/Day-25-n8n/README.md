# Day 25: Introduction to n8n

Day 25 introduced **n8n**, a workflow automation platform for connecting applications, APIs, data sources, and AI services. The session focused on understanding how automated workflows can support practical business and data tasks.

## Session Focus

- Understand the purpose of n8n and its role in workflow automation.
- Explore workflows, triggers, nodes, actions, and data flow between steps.
- Discuss how n8n can connect APIs, databases, spreadsheets, and AI services.
- Consider how generative AI can be included in automated workflows.
- Discover practical scenarios where automation can reduce repetitive manual work.
- Discuss the importance of validating data, handling errors, and protecting credentials.

## Instructor-Led Exploration

The scenarios were discovered and discussed with the instructor during the session. The focus was on understanding possible n8n use cases and the reasoning behind designing an automation workflow rather than completing a standalone implementation.
