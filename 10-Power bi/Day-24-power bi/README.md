# Day 24: Power BI Scenario Exploration

Day 24 was an instructor-led session focused on discovering and discussing different Power BI scenarios. The session connected Power BI concepts to practical business questions and helped explore how different reporting requirements can influence data preparation, modeling, visuals, and analysis.

## Session Focus

- Explore practical scenarios where Power BI can support business analysis.
- Discuss how to translate business questions into reports and dashboards.
- Consider suitable data sources, transformations, models, and visualizations for each scenario.
- Compare possible approaches and discuss the reasoning behind choosing them.
- Connect the scenarios to the Power BI workflow practiced in the previous days.

## Files

This folder contains only this README. No lab file, dataset, Power BI report, or other submitted deliverable was provided for Day 24 because the scenarios were discovered and discussed with the instructor during the session.

## Outcome

By the end of Day 24, I gained a broader understanding of how Power BI can be applied to different business scenarios and how the reporting approach should be selected based on the question, data, and expected audience.
