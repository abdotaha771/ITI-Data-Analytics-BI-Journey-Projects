# Module 03: Statistics

This module introduces the foundations of statistics for analytics and business intelligence. It focuses on descriptive statistics, probability concepts, sampling, and interpreting data patterns to support evidence-based decision-making.

---

## Learning Objectives by Day

- **Day 07 — Introduction to Statistics:**
  - Understanding the role of statistics in analytics and business decisions.
  - Differentiating between population and sample data.
  - Measuring central tendency: mean, median, and mode.
  - Measuring dispersion: range, variance, standard deviation, and coefficient of variation.
  - Interpreting frequency distributions and basic data summaries.
  - Applying descriptive statistics to real-world datasets.
- **Day 08 — Statistical Analysis and Interpretation:**
  - Understanding probability and basic probability distributions.
  - Applying correlation and regression concepts for data relationships.
  - Using statistical thinking for forecasting and business insight.
  - Interpreting results with confidence and practical business meaning.
  - Preparing analytical summaries for dashboards and reporting.
