# Day 08 — Statistical Analysis and Interpretation

This day builds on the basics of statistics and focuses on probability, relationships between variables, and how to meaningfully interpret analytical outcomes.

---

## Topics Covered

- Probability basics
- Normal distribution and common statistical assumptions
- Correlation between variables
- Linear regression concepts
- Statistical interpretation in business context
- Communicating results effectively for reporting and dashboards

---

## Objectives

- Understand how data relationships are analyzed statistically
- Interpret results beyond the numbers
- Connect statistical concepts to business value and analytics decisions
