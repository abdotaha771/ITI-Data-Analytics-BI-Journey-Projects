# Days 19, 20 & 21: Freelancing Track

These days cover the Mahara Tech freelancing track, focusing on freelance career preparation and the practical steps required to start working through online freelance platforms.

---

## Learning Objectives

- Understand the basics of freelancing and independent work.
- Find a freelance direction that matches personal skills and interests.
- Learn the initial steps for starting a freelance career.
- Explore how to get started on Khamsat, Mostaql, and Upwork.
- Prepare for presenting skills and services to potential clients.

---

## Track Topics

- Freelancing basics.
- Choosing a suitable freelance path.
- Building a foundation for a freelance profile.
- Getting started with Khamsat.
- Getting started with Mostaql.
- Getting started with Upwork.

---

## Outcome

By the end of Days 19, 20, and 21, learners should be able to describe the freelancing model, select a suitable freelance direction, and identify the practical steps for starting on major freelance platforms.
