# Day 12: Reporting Services (SSRS)

This day introduces SQL Server Reporting Services (SSRS) and its role in presenting business data through interactive and printable reports. The lab produces reports for students, courses, grades, product quantities, and SSAS results.

---

## Lab Material

- [BI_Lab3.docx](BI_Lab3.docx): Lab instructions and required SSRS reporting activities.

## Lab Assets

- `iti_image.jpeg`: Image asset used in the report design.
- `Report1_show_student.pdf`: Student information report.
- `Report2_course_by_dep.pdf`: Courses grouped by department.
- `Report3_quantity_per_product.pdf`: Quantity by product report.
- `Report4_student grades.pdf`: Student grades report.
- `Report5_courses data .pdf`: Courses data report.
- `Report6_student data.pdf`: Student data report.
- `Report7.pdf`: Additional SSRS report output.
- `Report8_SSAS.pdf`: Report based on SSAS data.

---

## Learning Focus

- Understanding the purpose of SSRS in business intelligence.
- Connecting reports to datasets and data sources.
- Designing reports for student, course, grade, and product data.
- Grouping and presenting data for easier analysis.
- Using images and report layouts to improve presentation.
- Validating report results against the underlying data.

---

## Outcome

By the end of the day, learners should be able to create basic SSRS reports, organize data into useful views, and present results from relational and analytical data sources.
