# Day 11: Analysis Services (SSAS)

This day introduces SQL Server Analysis Services (SSAS) and its role in building analytical models for business intelligence. The lab works with sales data and applies measures and KPI concepts to support analytical reporting.

---

## Lab Material

- [SSAS LAB.docx](SSAS%20LAB.docx): Lab instructions and required Analysis Services activities.

## Lab Assets

- `sales.bak`: Sales database backup used as the source for the analytical model.
- `task_4.xlsx`: Spreadsheet output or supporting data for the lab task.
- `kpi_task_2.xlsx`: KPI-related spreadsheet output.

---

## Learning Focus

- Understanding the purpose of SSAS in business intelligence.
- Preparing source data for an analytical model.
- Working with dimensions, measures, and calculated business metrics.
- Defining and interpreting KPIs.
- Validating analytical results against the source data.

---

## Outcome

By the end of the day, learners should be able to create or work with a basic SSAS analytical model, define meaningful measures and KPIs, and use the results for business reporting.
