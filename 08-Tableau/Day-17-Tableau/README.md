# Day 17: Tableau

This day applies Tableau to sales and profit analysis through interactive visualizations and dashboard exploration.

---

## Analysis Questions

The workbook answers the following business questions:

- Which region generates the highest sales, and is it also the most profitable?
- Which sub-category creates the largest loss?
- Which state has strong sales but weak or negative profit?
- Which segment contributes the most sales?
- What pattern appears in the monthly sales trend?

---

## Key Findings

- The West region generates the highest sales and is also the most profitable.
- Tables is the sub-category with the largest loss.
- Texas has strong sales but weak or negative profit.
- The Consumer segment contributes the most sales.
- Sales peak in the last quarter of the year, followed by a significant drop at the beginning of the following year.
