# Day 18: Advanced Tableau

This day focuses on advanced Tableau analysis for profit optimization, customer behavior, shipping performance, and management-ready dashboard insights.

---

## Analysis Questions

The dashboard answers the following management questions:

- Which Region/Sub-Category combinations have the weakest profit margin?
- Which high-sales customer is generating a loss?
- Which ship mode has the longest average shipping days?
- How does the ranking of sub-categories change between sales and profit?
- What actions should management take based on the dashboard findings?

---

## Key Findings

- East & Table has the weakest combined profit margin.
- Sean Miller is a high-sales customer with a negative profit outcome.
- Standard Class has the longest average shipping days.
- Sub-category rankings shift noticeably when switching between sales and profit views.
- Management should reduce discounts on loss-making high-sales accounts, re-evaluate pricing for Bookcases and Tables, and investigate furnishings profitability in the Central region.
